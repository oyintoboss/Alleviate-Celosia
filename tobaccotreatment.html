A. School Prevention Program - Life- skill training

The Botvin Life-skill Training (LST) is the outcome of over 30 years of rigorous work of Prof Gilbert J. Botvin. The trainings are majorly school based, evidence-driven, multiple outcomes proven and age specific substance use prevention training.

It is good to know about ills of substance use, but more importantly be skilled on how to say no or not to start the use of drugs.

Global coverage

Botvin Life-skill courses have been attended by over 3 million students worldwide in 10,000 sites trained by about 50, 000 teachers in 39 countries of the world.

The aim of the training is to prevent onset of use of drug / substance of abuse use in preteens and teenagers.

These goals would be achieved by training these skills:

  1. General Social skill development through

    *Communication Skills *Greetings and Brief Social Exchanges *Meeting New People *Conversational Skills *Complimenting Skills *Assertive Skills

  2. Personal self-management skills
  3. *Problem-Solving and Decision-Making *Personal Behaviour Change Skills * Stress and Anxiety Management
  4. Drug resistance skills

C. Tobacco Prevention, Empowerment, Treatment, Protection and Evaluation

Tobacco use complication is one of the leading causes of death worldwide. There is great need for its demand reduction since tobacco use does not only affect the direct smoker but there are cases of second and third hand smoke affecting innocent persons.

There is need to encourage those who smoke to consider quitting or give them safe alternatives. And give those desirous of treatment adequate care. This can only be achieved with professionals with sufficient knowledge of tobacco treatment.

D. Wellness Recovery Action Plan (WRAP)

Wellness Recovery Action Plan (WRAP) is a highly personalized, self-designed framework used to manage mental health, physical well-being, and life challenges. It was developed by people in recovery, it is an evidence-based system centred around five core concepts: hope, personal responsibility, education, self-advocacy, and support.
